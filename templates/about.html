Persian Heritage
01

For centuries, Persian rugs have represented one of the world’s most distinguished traditions of artistry, craftsmanship, and cultural expression. Each rug reflects the creativity of its makers, the character of its region, and the heritage passed through generations of Persian artisans.

02

With roots in Persian rug craftsmanship dating back to 1959, Keshmiri Rugs continues a tradition of expertise established through Farsh Bastani, a historic rug house founded in Tabriz, one of Persia’s most celebrated weaving centers.

Three Generations

Through generations of experience in weaving, trading, and international rug commerce, we have developed a deep understanding of the artistry, materials, and techniques behind exceptional Persian rugs.

Carefully Curated

Today, Keshmiri Rugs carefully curates handwoven pieces from across Persia, working directly with skilled weavers, trusted workshops, and established collectors.
